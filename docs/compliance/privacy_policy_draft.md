## 1. What This App Is

Bubble Reef Rush is a music rhythm game for children and families. You guide a friendly fish through an underwater reef by tapping and holding your screen in time with the music. The game is designed for kids ages 6 to 12 and their families.

We built Bubble Reef Rush to be a safe, fun experience — which means we collected as little information as possible about the people who play it. Please read this policy to understand exactly what we do and don't do with your information.

This policy applies to:
- The Bubble Reef Rush app on Android (Google Play)

## 3. Information We DO Collect

Here is exactly what is collected when you use Bubble Reef Rush.

### 3.1 Game Progress (Stored on Your Device)

When you play the game, we save your progress locally on your device. This includes:

- Which levels you have completed and your best score on each level
- How many stars you have earned on each level
- Which characters and cosmetic items you have unlocked
- Your total in-game coins
- The date you first completed each level

This information is stored in a file on your device (`save_data.json`). It stays on your device and is used only to let you pick up where you left off.

### 3.2 Cloud Save (Optional, via Platform Services)

If you are signed into Google Play Games (on Android) or Apple Game Center (on iOS), your game progress can be saved to the cloud so it carries over if you switch devices. This uses Google's and Apple's own game services — we do not run our own servers for this.

The data that gets synced to the cloud is the same game progress described in Section 3.1 above: your level scores, stars, unlocked items, and coins. No personal information is included.

Google and Apple assign a pseudonymous player ID (a code that identifies your game account without using your name or email) to make this sync work. We do not see or store your Google account email or Apple ID.

If you do not want cloud save, you can turn off Google Play Games or Game Center on your device, and your progress will stay only on your device.

### 3.3 In-App Purchase Receipts (via Platform Stores)

Bubble Reef Rush offers two optional one-time purchases:
- Unlock Full Reef ($2.99): unlocks Zones 4 and 5
- Creator Pass ($1.99): allows publishing custom levels

All payments are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or Apple App Store (iOS). **We never see your credit card number, billing address, or payment method.** The platform sends us only a confirmation that the purchase was completed so we can unlock the content you bought. This confirmation does not include any personal information about you.

### 3.4 Crash Reports (If a Crash Occurs)

If the app crashes, we may collect information about the crash to help us fix the problem. This could include:

- What kind of device you were using (e.g., "Android phone, OS version 14")
- What part of the game was running when it crashed
- A technical error log (no personal information is included in this log)

Crash data does not include your name, location, or any other personal information. It is used only to fix bugs.
