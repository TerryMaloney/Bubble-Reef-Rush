# Bubble Reef Rush — World Bible
**Narrative Designer Reference | C-3 | Ages 6–12**

---

## 1. The Premise

Pebble the pufferfish loves music more than anything. One magical morning, a giant wave of sound swept through the reef! Now every rock, coral, and shell is bouncing to the beat. Pebble wants to ride that beat all the way to the Crystal Caves — the most musical place in the whole ocean!

---

## 2. Zone Descriptions

### Zone 1 — Sunlit Shallows
The water here is warm and sparkly bright. Sunbeams dance on the sandy floor, and little fish dart between pink corals. Pebble dashes through because the first notes of the Big Wave started right here!

### Zone 2 — Kelp Forest Canyon
Tall green kelp sways like a slow, swishing curtain. Light filters down in soft green ribbons, and tiny bubbles pop like gentle drums. Pebble weaves between the kelp because the beat is getting louder and more exciting!

### Zone 3 — Shipwreck Alley
Old wooden ships rest quietly on the seafloor. Portholes glow with a golden light, and fish swim in and out like they own the place. Pebble zooms through because the shipwrecks echo the music in a super cool way!

### Zone 4 — Volcanic Vent Fields
Hot water bubbles up from cracks in the dark rock below. Glowing orange vents puff and pulse in time with a deep, booming rhythm. Pebble rushes through because the vents drum the beat harder than anywhere else!

### Zone 5 — Twilight Trench
This deep, deep place is dim and full of mystery. Tiny glowing creatures drift like floating stars, blinking on and off to a secret song. Pebble swims through carefully because this quiet rhythm leads the way to the Crystal Caves!

### Zone 6 — Crystal Caves (Secret)
Every wall shimmers with giant crystals that ring like bells. Rainbow light bounces everywhere, and the air hums with pure, perfect music. Pebble made it! This is where the Big Wave's heart beats loudest of all!

## 3. Character Bios

### Pebble
**Species:** Pufferfish (starter character)
Pebble is always trying their very best, even when things get tricky. They puff up with excitement — not fear — and never, ever give up. **Catchphrase:** "I can do this! Let's PUFF!"

### Zap
**Species:** Electric Eel
Zap crackles with energy and talks really, really fast. They love going first and lighting up every room — sometimes literally. **Catchphrase:** "ZAP to it!"

### Mochi
**Species:** Jellyfish
Mochi floats along in their own dreamy world, humming softly. They seem slow, but they always end up exactly where they need to be. **Catchphrase:** "Drift... and bloom!"

### Crusher
**Species:** Hermit Crab (lives in a tiny castle shell)
Crusher thinks their shell is the most impressive home in the sea. They stomp around proudly but secretly love cheering everyone else on. **Catchphrase:** "All hail the castle!"

### Pip
**Species:** Seahorse (very tiny)
Pip is the smallest one on the reef but has the biggest heart. They work twice as hard as anyone and are always the last to quit. **Catchphrase:** "Small but speedy!"

### Lumina
**Species:** Anglerfish
Lumina's glowing lure bobs and swirls like a tiny disco ball. They are calm, curious, and always shining a light on something new. **Catchphrase:** "Follow my glow!"

### Finn
**Species:** Blue Tang
Finn is cheerful, chatty, and always pointing out something amazing nearby. Sometimes they swim in circles by accident, but they have great fun doing it. **Catchphrase:** "Did you see THAT?!"

### Grumble
**Species:** Old Sea Turtle
Grumble acts grumpy but secretly loves the music more than any of them. They have seen every part of the reef and know all the best shortcuts. **Catchphrase:** "Fine. Let's swim."

---

## 4. The Beat

Long ago, the ocean learned a secret: music makes everything better. The reef remembered every song it ever heard. Now, when a Big Wave of music rolls through, the reef wakes up and dances! Corals clap. Rocks roll. Currents swirl and spin. The things that pop up in Pebble's path are not enemies — they are part of the song. Every jump, every dodge, every perfect move is Pebble playing along. The whole reef is one giant living instrument, and Pebble is its star performer!
